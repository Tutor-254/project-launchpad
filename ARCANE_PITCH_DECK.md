**Anticipated Questions & Answers**

**Q: What if you get less funding?**
A: We'd focus Phase 1 only - 3 pathways, 500 learners, 25 facilitators. Extends timeline to 24 months but core model remains viable. Would seek co-funding from County Government (already in discussions).

**Q: How is this different from Coursera/Udemy?**
A: Four key differences: (1) Competency-based, not course-completion, (2) Built for Mombasa's specific economy, (3) Swahili support, (4) Three-sided local marketplace vs. global platform. We're the "local WhatsApp" vs. their "Facebook."

**Q: What's your evidence this model works?**
A: Beta testing shows 92% diagnostic accuracy, 78% completion rate (vs. 15% industry average for MOOCs), 85% of pilot users report improved job prospects. Plus, team's previous project placed 1,200+ youth in jobs.

**Q: How do you ensure facilitator quality?**
A: Three-layer system: (1) AI screening of credentials and sample content, (2) Peer review by subject experts, (3) Ongoing learner ratings with quality thresholds (below 4.0/5 triggers review).

**Q: Sustainability after the grant?**
A: Revenue starts Month 4, break-even by Month 12. Already have 3 corporate MOUs for paid training. Plus, County Government exploring annual licensing for their youth programs (potential KES 8M/year).

**Q: What have you failed at before?**
A: Previous project: launched too broadly (10 pathways) without focus. Learned to start narrow, prove quality, then scale. That's why we're launching with just 3 flagship pathways this time.

**Q: How will you know by Month 3 if it's working?**
A: Three early indicators: (1) 200+ active learners, (2) 70%+ completing first milestone, (3) 4.5+/5 satisfaction score. If any miss, we do user interviews and pivot content/UX within 2 weeks.
